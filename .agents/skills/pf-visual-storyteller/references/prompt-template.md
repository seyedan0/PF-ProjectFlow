```text
Technical visual diagram in hand-drawn digital ink and clean technical line art style, 16:9 aspect ratio.
Theme: Deep forest obsidian dark mode (#070D0A background), high contrast with crisp white/light gray lines (#F8FAFC) and exactly 2-3 controlled semantic accents: emerald green (#10B981) for validated flow, amber (#F59E0B) for filters/gates, and rose (#F43F5E) for boundaries/drift.
Composition Pattern: [Insert Composition Pattern: Sieve/Funnel | Split-Screen | Bridge/Adapter | Control Loop | Iceberg | Hub and Orbit].
Visual Metaphor: [Describe concrete physical metaphor, e.g. A multi-stage mechanical sieve separating noisy chaotic code inputs into a single glowing green capsule].
Typography: Minimalist technical labels in JetBrains Mono font, max 5-7 concise labels.
Negative Space: At least 35% open whitespace, perfectly balanced, zero visual clutter, flat fills with 12% opacity.
```
